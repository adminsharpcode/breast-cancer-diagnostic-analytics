Decision Tree Boundaries

Partitions space strictly parallel to coordinate axes. Creates rigid rectangular step boundaries that can easily overfit or miss slanted diagonal separations in biological cell growth.

Linear SVM Hyperplane

Maximizes the separating margin width $2/\|\mathbf{w}\|$ via convex quadratic programming. Separates the global masses effectively but cannot curve around overlapping peripheral outliers.

Non-Linear RBF Kernel (Optimal)

Maps instances into an infinite-dimensional RKHS with Gaussian kernel bandwidth $\gamma=0.01$. Envelopes complex non-convex malignant clusters smoothly, producing the highest clinical sensitivity.
